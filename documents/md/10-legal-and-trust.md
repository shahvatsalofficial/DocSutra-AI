## Privacy

No telemetry. No network calls during a scan. No AI-provider calls. Reports stay where you write them. Credentials are redacted before storage. You remain responsible for the documents you scan and for sharing reports.

## Claims policy

This project avoids unverifiable claims. It does not say "world-class", "most accurate", "secure" or "certified". It states measured facts with a method and a limit. See [Testing and evidence](07-testing-and-evidence.md).

## What a report proves

| A passing report means | A passing report does not mean |
|---|---|
| The listed static checks passed on the files at that time | Documentation claims are true |
| No credential in the supported formats was found | No secret exists |
| Local links and anchors resolve | External links work |
| Documented flags exist in the contract you declared | The real CLI has those flags |
