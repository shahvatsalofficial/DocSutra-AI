Legal and trust

Licensing, names, privacy, what a report does and does not prove. Not legal advice.

Dependencies

There are no runtime or development dependencies. Only Node.js built-in modules are used, so there are no third-party licences to track.

Privacy

No telemetry. No network calls during a scan. No AI-provider calls. Reports stay where you write them. Credentials are redacted before storage. You remain responsible for the documents you scan and for sharing reports.

Claims policy

This project avoids unverifiable claims. It does not say "world-class", "most accurate", "secure" or "certified". It states measured facts with a method and a limit. See Testing and evidence.
